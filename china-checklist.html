🚀 Why These Things Matter

A lot of first-time visitors assume China works exactly like Europe, North America, or Australia. It doesn't. That's not a bad thing — it's just different. Here's what catches people off guard:

📱
Mobile Payments Rule Everything
Coffee, train tickets, street food, taxis — all paid by QR code. Setting up Alipay and WeChat Pay before you go removes the biggest pain point of China travel.
📶
The Internet Is Different
Many travelers only realize Google, Instagram, and WhatsApp are blocked after they land. Install and test your VPN before departure — not after. Future You will be grateful.
🗺️
Download Offline Tools
Even with perfect mobile data, download offline maps anyway. Technology loves failing when you're dragging a suitcase through a train station the size of a small country.
⚠️
The One Thing You Cannot Fix After Landing

VPN. Most VPN websites are blocked in China, which means you cannot install one after you arrive. This is the single most important item on the entire checklist. Do it first, do it at home, test it before you fly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I prepare for a China trip?
Ideally 2–4 weeks before departure. That gives you enough time for visas, payment app setup, and Wise card delivery. The VPN and apps can be done in a single afternoon — but don't leave it to the last day.
Can I use Google Maps in China?
Sometimes — with a VPN. But Baidu Maps is generally far more reliable for navigating inside China. Download it before you go and save key locations offline.
Do I need a VPN before I land in China?
Yes — absolutely. Most VPN websites are blocked inside China, which means you cannot download or set one up after arrival. Install it, subscribe, and test it before you board the plane.
Is it safe to use foreign cards in China?
Yes, especially through Alipay and WeChat Pay which both now support foreign Visa and Mastercard. Wise also works well for ATM withdrawals. Always bring a backup card just in case.
What's the single most important thing to prepare for China?
VPN first. Alipay second. WeChat third. Those three tools solve roughly 90% of the challenges first-time travelers face in China. Everything else on this checklist is important — those three are essential.
